Small multilingual support does not guarantee accurate recognition of accents, noisy recordings or every language. Local synthetic English and Chinese smoke tests passed; Malay and real customer audio quality remain unverified. The Kokoro voice was checked by machine only (loudness, pauses, speech recognition of its output), not by listening; its Chinese voices read English words with a noticeable accent. Recognition probabilities are model signals, not calibrated guarantees. Uncertain cuts must remain protected. Word timing is Whisper-estimated; forced alignment/diarization and translation are not included.

WhisperX aligners/diarization, OpenCLIP, MediaPipe and Demucs models are **not shipped** by this integration. Of PaddleOCR, only the two PP-OCRv6 small ONNX models listed above are shipped; no Paddle runtime, angle classifier or other OCR language model is included. Review the exact checkpoint's license/model card/dataset conditions before adopting it; the library license alone does not authorize arbitrary weight redistribution. The older speech/GUI installers do not include the new tools; see [tools edition scope](docs/offline-tools.zh-CN.md).
